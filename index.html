DicteeQuest

DicteeQuest is an iPad app for French Immersion students. Parents type in or scan the weekly dictation list from school, and children go on quests with a little fox: they listen to the French, write by hand on the screen, see their own spelling next to the correct one when they make a mistake, and keep practising until they can write every word and sentence from memory on their own.

  • Every real try earns stars and stickers. Mistakes are never punished.
  • All data stays on the device. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.
  • Purchases and outside links are behind a parental gate.
