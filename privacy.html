DicteeQuest 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 25 日

DicteeQuest 是一款帮助孩子练习法语听写的 iPad App。我们把孩子的隐私放在第一位：App 不收集、不上传任何个人数据。

我们不收集什么

  • 没有账号、不需要注册或登录。
  • 没有广告，没有数据统计或分析，没有任何第三方 SDK。
  • App 自身不连接我们的服务器（我们也没有服务器）。

数据存在哪里

  • 孩子的名字（或昵称）、头像表情、词表、手写笔迹图片、练习记录、星星和贴纸等，只保存在这台设备上。
  • 如果您开启了 iPad 系统的 iCloud 云备份或电脑备份，这些数据可能会随整机备份一起由 Apple 系统保存；这是系统功能，我们无法访问。
  • 为了执行免费版规则，App 会在设备钥匙串里保存一个数字（累计新建了几份词表），不含任何个人信息，也不同步到 iCloud。
  • 删除 App 会删除 App 内的全部数据（上面这个计数除外，它不含个人信息）。

相机和照片

  • 相机和相册只用来拍下或选择老师发的词表，在设备上用 Apple 的 Vision 文字识别读出单词。
  • 照片不会离开设备，也不会被上传。

语音、例句和翻译

  • 法语朗读使用 iPad 系统自带的语音，在设备上完成。
  • 练习例句由 App 内置模板或设备上的 Apple 智能模型生成，在设备上完成。
  • 单词翻译使用 Apple 系统的翻译功能，在设备上完成。
  • 高质量语音或翻译语言包需要时由 iPad 系统从 Apple 下载，这个过程由系统处理，App 不发送您的内容。

备份文件

  • 只有当您在家长端主动点“导出备份文件”时，App 才会生成备份文件（包含词表、练习记录和字帖墙图片）。
  • 文件通过系统分享面板交给您，由您自己决定保存在哪里；我们收不到这个文件。

购买

  • 完整版是一次性购买，由 Apple 的 App Store 处理付款。App 拿不到您的姓名、支付方式或其他付款信息，只知道这台设备的 Apple 账户是否已解锁完整版。

面向儿童

  • App 是给孩子用的。购买、恢复购买和所有打开外部网页的链接，都要先通过家长验证（一道两位数乘两位数的算术题）。
  • 孩子使用的界面不显示价格和购买按钮。

政策变更与联系方式

如果本政策有变化，我们会更新本页面和生效日期。如有任何问题，请发邮件至 wenpeizou@gmail.com。

DicteeQuest Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026

DicteeQuest is an iPad app that helps children practise French dictation. Children's privacy comes first: the app does not collect or upload any personal data.

What we don't collect

  • No accounts. No sign-up or sign-in.
  • No ads, no analytics or tracking, and no third-party SDKs.
  • The app does not connect to any server of ours (we don't have one).

Where your data lives

  • Your child's name (or nickname), avatar emoji, word lists, handwriting images, practice history, stars and stickers are stored only on this device.
  • If you have turned on iCloud Backup or computer backups for your iPad, this data may be included in your device backup, which is handled by Apple's system. We cannot access it.
  • To apply the free-version rules, the app keeps one number in the device Keychain (how many word lists have been created). It contains no personal information and is not synced to iCloud.
  • Deleting the app deletes all of its data (except the number above, which contains no personal information).

Camera and photos

  • The camera and photo library are only used to capture or choose the word list from school. The words are read on the device using Apple's Vision text recognition.
  • Photos never leave the device and are never uploaded.

Speech, practice sentences and translation

  • French read-aloud uses the iPad's built-in voices and runs on the device.
  • Practice sentences are created from built-in templates or by Apple's on-device intelligence model, on the device.
  • Word translations use Apple's system translation and run on the device.
  • When a high-quality voice or a translation language pack is needed, iPadOS downloads it from Apple. The system handles this; the app does not send your content.

Backup files

  • A backup file (word lists, practice history and handwriting wall images) is created only when you tap “Export Backup File” in the parent area.
  • The file is handed to you through the system share sheet, and you decide where to keep it. We never receive it.

Purchases

  • The full version is a one-time purchase handled by Apple's App Store. The app never sees your name, payment method or other payment details. It only learns whether the full version is unlocked for the Apple Account on this device.

Designed for children

  • Buying, restoring purchases and every link that opens a web page are behind a parental gate (a two-digit by two-digit multiplication question).
  • Screens children use show no prices and no buy buttons.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Questions? Email wenpeizou@gmail.com.
